About Me

I'm a data visualization engineer who helps leaders in education and other mission-driven organizations find better answers to support decision-making.

I've found that many organizations rely on experience and intuition when making decisions, often because data is scattered across fragmented systems or simply buried in messy spreadsheets. Public data sources add another layer of complexity, with inconsistent quality and depth, forcing decision-makers to turn to commercial vendors to find answers.

I help close that gap by transforming untapped raw data into clear, compelling visualizations so leaders can make better decisions
